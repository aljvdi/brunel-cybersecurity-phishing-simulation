    Before you click, remember:
    • Check the sender's full address and the actual link destination.
    • Be wary of pressure, unexpected payments and requests for codes.
    • Go to trusted services yourself rather than following email links.
    • Report suspicious messages using Outlook's Report Phishing feature.
